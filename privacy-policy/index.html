Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

UncleDrum: Groove Lab ("UncleDrum") is developed by Funky Frog Labs.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use UncleDrum on iOS or Android.

1. Information Stored Locally

UncleDrum stores information needed to provide its features locally on your device. This may include rhythm patterns, folders, training data, app preferences, instrument volumes, tutorial progress, cached Premium status, and the remaining duration and daily usage of temporary ad-free rewards.

UncleDrum does not currently require an account. Funky Frog Labs does not directly request your name, email address, phone number, payment card details, or precise location through the app.

2. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads SDK

The free version of UncleDrum may display banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements provided through Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. A rewarded advertisement is shown only after you choose to watch it. Completing one grants 30 minutes without banner advertisements.

Rewarded ad-free periods do not accumulate: another rewarded advertisement is unavailable while a temporary ad-free period remains active. A new installation also receives one initial 15-minute ad-free period without consuming the daily rewarded-ad allowance.

Depending on your region and privacy choices, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited advertisements. Premium users and users with an active temporary ad-free reward are not intended to receive advertising requests from UncleDrum.

According to Google's SDK disclosures, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as IP address, general location inferred from IP address, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, advertising data, interactions with advertisements, app interactions, diagnostic information, crash logs, and performance data. The information actually processed depends on the device, region, consent choices, SDK configuration, and advertising mode.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy practices. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Advertising Privacy Choices

UncleDrum uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and store advertising privacy choices where required. UMP determines whether a privacy message needs to be shown and whether the app may request advertisements.

If you consent, advertisements may be personalized. If you do not consent to personalization, Google may serve non-personalized or limited advertisements when permitted. Declining personalization does not activate Premium or remove the advertising area from the free version.

Where required, you can review or change your choices using the Privacy options entry in the app's settings. Choices are stored and interpreted by Google's UMP SDK according to the applicable message and region.

4. Premium and In-App Purchases

UncleDrum offers a non-consumable Premium purchase. Purchases, payments, transaction history, refunds, and restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play, depending on your platform. Funky Frog Labs does not receive or store your full payment card information.

The app receives transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock Premium and restore the purchase. The app may cache the resulting Premium status locally for faster access. Apple and Google process purchase information under their respective privacy policies.

For more information, see App Store & Privacy and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. How Information Is Used and Shared

Locally stored information is used to provide UncleDrum's core functionality, remember settings, save rhythm content, enforce free-version limits, and maintain Premium or temporary ad-free state.

Funky Frog Labs does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers described in this policy when necessary to provide advertising, consent management, purchases, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and related platform services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because UncleDrum does not currently use app accounts, locally stored app data can generally be removed by deleting the app or clearing its storage in your device settings. A purchase record may remain associated with your App Store or Google Play account so that Premium can be restored.

Google, Apple, and Google Play retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. To exercise rights regarding information processed by those services, use the privacy controls or contact methods they provide.

7. Children's Privacy

UncleDrum is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Funky Frog Labs, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect app-related information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when UncleDrum's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
